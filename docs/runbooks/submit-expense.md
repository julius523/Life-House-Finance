# Submit an expense

## When to use this
Any time you spent Life House money (your card, cash you're owed back, or a card on file) and need it recorded in the books.

## Prerequisites
- You're signed in as a submitter, approver, or admin.
- You have the receipt as a photo or PDF (preferred). It's still possible to submit without one — see "Without a receipt" below.

## Step-by-step (with a receipt)
1. From the sidebar, click **Expenses**, then **New expense** (top-right).
2. Fill in **Date**, **Vendor**, **Amount**, and pick the **Category** that matches the spend (e.g. "Office supplies", "Resident food").
3. Pick the **Program** the spend belongs to. If it's organisation-wide overhead, pick **General / Admin**.
4. Drop the receipt photo or PDF into the **Receipt** uploader. You'll see a preview once it finishes.
5. Add a one-line **Description** — what was bought and why. Keep it factual.
6. Click **Submit for approval**. The status will change to **Pending review**.

## Step-by-step (without a receipt)
1. Same as above through step 3.
2. Skip the receipt uploader. Tick **No receipt available** and pick the reason (lost, electronic transaction, donation in kind, etc.).
3. In **Description**, write what was bought, where, and approximately when. Be more detailed than usual — the approver will need this.
4. Submit. Expect more back-and-forth from your approver.

## What success looks like
- The expense appears in your **Expenses** list with status **Pending review**.
- The approver gets notified.
- Once approved, status flips to **Approved** and the expense is posted to the general ledger automatically.

## Common errors
- **"Period is locked"** — the month you picked has already been closed. Pick a date in the current open period or talk to an admin.
- **"Receipt failed to upload"** — file is over 10 MB or not an allowed type (we accept images, PDF, plain text, CSV). Re-export as PDF and try again.
- **"Vendor not found"** — type the name; if it's new, you can create it inline. Don't create duplicates — search first.

## Escalate to
Your approver, then `#finance-ops` if no response within one business day.
