V1 Advanced Layer — Additional Finish Phases
Phase 7 — Advanced AP Subledger
Goal

Turn bills/AP from “bill records that generate journal drafts” into a true AP subledger with aging, vendor balances, payment status, credit memos, partial payments, and reconciliation to the general ledger.

Task 14 — AP Subledger Core Tables

What & Why
Bills exist, but we need a reliable AP subledger that tracks what is owed, what has been paid, what is overdue, and how it ties to the ledger.

Done looks like

Each vendor has an AP balance.
Each bill has:
original amount
open balance
paid amount
status: draft, submitted, approved, posted, partially_paid, paid, voided
due date
aging bucket
Partial payments are supported.
Vendor credits / credit memos are supported.
AP subledger total reconciles to the AP liability account in the ledger.

Hard gates

No payment can exceed the open bill balance.
Voided bills cannot be paid.
Paid bills cannot be edited except through reversal/adjustment workflows.
AP subledger total must tie to the AP account in Trial Balance.
Task 15 — Bill Payment Workflow

What & Why
Once bills are posted to AP, the app needs a controlled way to record payments without bypassing accounting controls.

Done looks like

Admin/Finance can record a payment against one or more bills.
Payment creates a draft JE:
Debit AP
Credit Cash / Bank / Credit Card / Clearing
Supports partial payments.
Supports one payment paying multiple bills.
Payment draft goes through the same JE approval workflow.
Bill balances update only after the payment JE is posted.

Hard gates

No direct ledger post.
No silent payment status update before JE post.
Payment draft must link back to bill(s).
Payment reversal restores bill open balance correctly.

Phase 10 — Autonomous AI Actions, Safely

This is the risky one. Yes, you can include it in V1, but it must be staged.

The rule should be:

AI can prepare, suggest, draft, classify, flag, and queue. AI cannot silently post, approve, delete, pay, or override controls.

Task 25 — AI Action Policy Engine

What & Why
Before AI can do anything autonomous, the app needs rules that define what AI is allowed to do.

Done looks like
Create an AI action policy matrix:

Allowed autonomous actions:

classify receipt
suggest expense category
suggest CoA mapping
flag duplicate
create draft memo
create draft JE from approved source if deterministic mapping exists
create anomaly finding
prepare explanation
suggest remediation

Human approval required:

submit JE
approve JE
post JE
mark not applicable
dismiss anomaly
change vendor master data
change program master data
record payment
reverse JE
remediate posted entries

Never allowed:

approve its own draft
post directly to ledger
delete audit history
change closed periods
bypass idempotency
bypass role permissions
alter source documents

Hard gates

Every AI action has:
action type
actor = AI service identity
human reviewer when required
source evidence
confidence
reason
audit log
